# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
An exploratory data analysis (EDA) and visualization project analyzing e-commerce transaction records (`E commerce.csv`) to uncover key revenue drivers, customer segment behaviors, product category performance, and geographical trends.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Python**
* **Pandas & NumPy:** Data cleaning, multi-condition filtering, grouping, and aggregation.
* **Matplotlib:** Custom-styled bar and horizontal bar charts with formatted data labels.

## 📊 Key Business Insights
* **Customer Segments:** **Regular customers** are the primary revenue engine, generating **27,296 orders** and **$1,914,854** in total sales, significantly outperforming New ($1,196,104) and VIP ($333,872) segments[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
* **Product Categories:** **Electronics** dominates the catalog, pulling in **$1,751,037** in revenue—more than triple the second-highest category, Accessories ($553,023)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
* **Geographical Performance:** **Tehran** leads all markets by a wide margin with **$963,907** in sales, followed by Mashhad ($438,112) and Karaj ($350,173)[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
* **Temporal Trends:** Sales activity peaked in **May 2025**, marking the highest-performing month across the dataset timeline[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Retention & Loyalty:** Focus marketing budgets on retaining the high-value **Regular customer base** while creating targeted conversion campaigns to move New customers into the Regular tier.
2. **Inventory & Regional Focus:** Prioritize stock availability and promotional campaigns for **Electronics** and concentrate logistics and ad spend in top-performing cities like **Tehran**.
